Introduction

Global Logic Media (“we,” “our,” “us,” or “the Agency”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website (https://www.globallogicmedia.com/), contact us, request our services, or interact with our digital marketing campaigns.

Our services may include digital marketing, search engine optimisation (SEO), social media marketing, website design and development, Google Ads, Meta Ads, paid advertising, content creation, graphic design, branding, and lead generation.

By using our website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it before processing your personal information.
